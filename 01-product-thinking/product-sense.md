## What is Product Sense?

Product Sense is the ability to understand **users, their needs, problems, goals, and context**, and use that understanding to make good product decisions.

It helps a PM answer questions like:

- What problem should we solve?
- Who should we solve it for?
- Why does the problem matter?
- What solution could address it?
- What trade-offs should we consider?
- How would we know if the solution worked?

Product Sense is not about memorizing frameworks.

It is about **structured product thinking and sound judgment**.

---

## Why PMs Need Product Sense

A PM constantly makes decisions with incomplete information.

Product Sense helps a PM:

- Understand user needs
- Identify meaningful problems
- Separate important problems from minor ones
- Generate useful solutions
- Prioritize opportunities
- Consider business and technical constraints
- Make trade-offs
- Think through the complete user experience
